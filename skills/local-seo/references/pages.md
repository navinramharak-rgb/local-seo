# Service and area pages

**One page per thing you want to rank for.** A single page listing six services ranks for none of them.

## Service pages

One per service that actually makes money. Not one per service you technically offer.

Structure:
- H1: the service and the city, written like a person. "Mobile Car Detailing in Edmonton"
- What it is and who it is for, in the first paragraph, with the city in it naturally
- What is included, specifically
- What it costs, or the range, or how pricing works. A page with no price at all loses to one with a range.
- How long it takes and what happens on the day
- Photos of that actual service, done by them
- Two or three reviews mentioning that service
- The questions people ask about it
- One clear way to get in touch, repeated at the bottom

Title tag formula: `[Service] in [City] | [Business]`: under about 60 characters.

## Area pages

Only worth building if there is something real to say about each one. Six near-identical suburb pages with the name swapped is a recognised spam pattern and Google collapses them into one.

Real content per area means: jobs actually done there, photos from there, a review from someone there, what is specific about the area (older homes, hard water, parking, the drive time).

If they cannot fill that for an area, do not build the page. Better to have three strong area pages than eleven thin ones.

## The homepage

- The city in the title tag
- The city in the H1 or the first paragraph
- Address in **text**, not inside an image, matching the profile character for character
- Tap-to-call phone in the header: `<a href="tel:+17805551234">`
- A map or directions
- Hours
- A link to the Google Business Profile

## NAP consistency

Name, address, phone. Identical everywhere: website, profile, every directory, every social bio.

Identical means identical. These all count as different:
- `Suite 4` / `#4` / `Unit 4`
- `Street` / `St.` / `St`
- `(780) 555-1234` / `780-555-1234` / `+1 780 555 1234`

Pick one format, write it down, use it everywhere. Fixing existing inconsistency matters more than adding new listings.

## Contact page

- The address in text
- A map
- Tap-to-call and a mailto
- Hours
- A form that asks for the minimum. Every extra field costs replies.
- What happens next and how fast. "We reply within one business day" outperforms a bare form.
